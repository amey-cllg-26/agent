GCOEK 10-Point CGPA Conversion Guide

Understanding your Shivaji University / DBATU grading scale for global admissions

Most international scholarship programs (USA, Germany, UK, Canada, Australia) require converting your 10-point cumulative grade point average (CGPA) into a standard 4.0 scale or a percentage:

GCOEK CGPA (10.0 Scale) Equivalent Percentage (%) Equivalent US GPA (4.0 Scale) Classification
9.0 – 10.0 85% – 100% 3.80 – 4.00 Outstanding / Top Merit
8.0 – 8.99 75% – 84% 3.30 – 3.79 First Class with Distinction
7.0 – 7.99 65% – 74% 2.80 – 3.29 First Class
6.0 – 6.99 55% – 64% 2.30 – 2.79 Higher Second Class

Tip for GCOEK Students: For German (DAAD/TUM) applications, the *Bavarian Formula* is standard: German Grade = 1 + 3 * (10 - Your_CGPA) / (10 - 4.0).

GCOEK Student Application Timeline

From 5th semester preparation to international departure

  1. Semester 5 (3rd Year): Focus on maintaining high CGPA (> 8.0). Begin preparation for GRE and IELTS/TOEFL. Apply for summer internships (DAAD WISE, IITs, or research labs).
  2. Semester 6 (3rd Year): Take GRE and IELTS/TOEFL exams. Shortlist target universities and scholarships from the GCOEK directory. Draft your Statement of Purpose (SOP).
  3. Semester 7 (Final Year): Request 3 Letters of Recommendation (LORs) from GCOEK professors. Submit applications before scholarship deadlines (typically October to January).
  4. Semester 8 (Final Year): Receive admission offers, apply for student visas, obtain official degree certificate/transcripts from GCOEK exam section, and attend orientation!
