GCOEK Mandatory Documents

For MahaDBT scholarship approval, keep these documents ready:

  • Income Certificate: Issued by Tahsildar for FY 2025-26.
  • Caste & Validity: Compulsory for SC, ST, VJNT, OBC, SBC.
  • Non-Creamy Layer: Valid for OBC, VJNT, SBC.
  • Domicile: Maharashtra state domicile certificate.
  • Aadhaar Link: Aadhaar seeded with active bank account (NPCI mapped).
